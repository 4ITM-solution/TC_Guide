Creator Brief · Get Ready for Summer ☀️

Summer-Ready Skin — Peeling Gel + Peeling Pad

A face & body story arc: from "summer is coming" to "instantly smoother, brighter skin." Eight beats, playful tone, with a clear before/after reveal as the payoff.

Face ProductDr. G Brightening Peeling Gel
Body ProductDr. G Brightening Vita Peeling Pad
Concept"Get Ready for Summer" · Face + Body care duo · Quick, glowy, sleeveless-ready
1Summer hook → 2Concern → 3Problem zoom → 4Face care → 5Body care → 6Glow proof → 7B&A → 8Close
Step 01

Hook — Summer Is Coming

Hook
"Time for sleeveless tops and shorts!"
VisualQuick, energetic shot — pulling out a tank top or summer dress from the closet, sun-soaked window light, holding it up against yourself.
Summer is here — light, breezy energy to open. Quick cuts of a wardrobe, sunlight streaming in, establishing the season fast.
Step 02

Raise the Concern

Concern
"But… my skin? Dull. Bumpy. Not summer-ready."
VisualFace turns a little worried — gently touching the cheek, glancing at shoulders or arms in the mirror. Small "ugh" reaction.
Dull skin, rough texture, little bumps popping up — honestly showing off that "my skin did not get the summer memo" situation. Keep it light, a little playful, not dramatic.
Step 03

Zoom in on the Problem

Detail
"See? Dry patches. Rough elbows. Knees. Shoulders."
VisualTight close-ups of dry, non-glowy areas — a cycle of: face → elbows → knees → shoulders. Slightly desaturated or flat lighting to make the "before" feel honest.
Zooming out beyond the face — elbows, knees, shoulders all getting their moment — so the case for a face-and-body duo writes itself.
Step 04

Face Care — Peeling Gel

Face
"For my face, I gently exfoliate with peeling gel and water. Super hydrated thanks to the rich hyaluronic acid."
VisualPump the gel into the palm, mix with a little water, massage onto the face in soft circles. Close-up on the rolling/peeling moment. Rinse — show plump, freshly hydrated skin.
"Gentle + with water" as the throughline. Positioning this as care that exfoliates without irritation while locking in moisture. Hyaluronic acid hydration front and center.
Step 05

Body Care — Peeling Pad

Body
"For my body, I swipe with peeling pads — quick care. With Vitamin C, for refreshed, luminous skin."
VisualOpen the jar, grab one pad, swipe over elbows, knees, shoulders, décolletage. Show the pad texture and the wipe motion. Quick & effortless feel.
Body gets the "30 seconds, done" energy. Vitamin C doing the heavy lifting on that instant brightness promise. Edit it faster than the face segment — the contrast in pace does the talking.
Step 06

Brighten & Instant Glow

Glow
"Look at this glow — instantly."
VisualCatch the light on the cheek, shoulder, or collarbone. Lightly tap or run a finger across smooth skin. Optional: angle the camera so light reflects off the now-glowing surface.
A shot that visually proves "instant radiance" — catch the moment light hits the skin and the effect sells itself.
Tip: Contains Vitamin C — with consistent use, skin tone gradually brightens over time. Instant glow now, long-term tone-up as the payoff.
Step 07

Before & After Reveal

B&A
"Smoother. Brighter. Glow turned ON."
VisualSide-by-side or split-screen B&A: face + body areas (elbow/shoulder/knee). Same lighting, same angle on both sides — that's what sells the dramatic difference.
This is the climax of the video. Same lighting, same angle — that's what makes the before-and-after land hardest. Show the contrast for both face and body — that side-by-side is the whole point.
Step 08

Playful Close

Outro
"Simple, quick, and perfect for summer-ready skin!"
VisualStep out in the sleeveless top or summer outfit teased in Step 1. Cheeky pose, wink, sunglasses on, or quick spin. Confident outdoor closing shot.
Close it by actually wearing the outfit teased in the opening — that callback seals the story arc cleanly. Tone stays light, confident.

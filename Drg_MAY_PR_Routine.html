PM Routine · Evening

Slow Down. Rebuild Your Glow.

One-step exfoliation that brightens, hydrates & boosts your skincare absorption.
Step 01 · PM

Start with PM Routine

"After a long day — let's slow it down with a real reset."
VisualCalm bathroom or vanity setting. Soft warm light. Hair clipped back, freshly cleansed face. Settle-in mood.
Evening shift. The quiet ritual of winding down — care that starts where the day ends.
Step 02 · PM

Using Peeling Gel

"One step that exfoliates, brightens, hydrates — and boosts everything you put on after."
VisualWet the face first — or use straight in the shower. Pump or squeeze the gel into the palm. Massage in gentle circles across the face — close-up on the rolling/peeling moment. Rinse off.
Key Points① Close-up on the gel's texture as it applies ② The moment exfoliation lifts — or freshly rinsed, visibly cleaner skin ③ Essence or serum absorbing smoothly right after ④ Use after wetting the face first — or works in the shower too
The core message: one-step exfoliation that boosts absorption — not just removing dead skin, but priming it so everything you layer on after works harder.
Step 03 · PM

Show the Result with Glowy Skin

"Skin that drinks everything in. Soft, glowy, ready for sleep."
VisualClose-up of bare, glowy skin under warm light. Touch the cheek lightly to show smooth texture. Optional: pat in a serum and show how fast it absorbs.
Glow check. The goal: leave the impression that skin looks its best right before bed.
☀️
AM Routine · Morning

Fast. Easy. Glow Before Makeup.

The pad version of your peeling gel — for mornings when you have 30 seconds.
Step 04 · AM

Transition to AM Routine

"Morning hits different. No time for steps — but I still want the glow."
VisualQuick scene change to bright, natural morning light. Could be a hard cut, a swipe transition, or curtains opening. Energy lifts.
The visual shift needs to land clearly — calm, unhurried PM energy giving way to a crisp, get-up-and-go AM tone.
Step 05 · AM

Using Peeling Pad

"Same exfoliation, in pad form. Swipe and go — straight into makeup."
VisualOpen the jar, grab one pad, swipe across the face quickly but thoroughly — linger on rough or congested areas like the nose and chin. Show the texture of the pad. Cut to the same areas post-swipe: smoother, pores visibly cleaner. Then cut directly to applying foundation/sunscreen — showing how it sits evenly on skin that's just been cleared.
Key Points① Pulling out a single pad ② Working it across problem areas — nose, chin, anywhere rough or congested ③ Skin right after: smoother, the texture visibly settled ④ Straight into makeup — foundation sitting evenly on freshly cleared skin
The pad is the gel, made faster. Same exfoliation, less steps — the point is to show the full flow in one routine: clear the skin, then watch the makeup lock in.
Step 06 · AM

Show the Result with Glowy Skin

"Smooth canvas, glow already on. Makeup just glides on top."
VisualBright daylight close-up. Show makeup applying smoothly — or finished AM look with clear, glowy skin. Confident outdoor shot to close.
Morning glow means one thing visually: a base so smooth that makeup has nowhere to go but on perfectly. Mirror the PM glow shot — same skin, just brighter and lighter — and the two routines connect on their own.
